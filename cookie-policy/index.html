Last Updated: September 21, 2026 Browser Storage & Technologies
Key Summary: FreeFireNicknamePro.com does not set tracking or advertising cookies. Our website uses standard client-side browser localStorage exclusively to save your favorited nicknames, symbols, and recently copied items so you don't lose them when you refresh the page.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

When you visit modern websites, data may be stored on or retrieved from your browser, primarily in the form of cookies or local web storage:

  • Cookies: Small text files placed on your device by a web server. Cookies are traditionally sent back to the server with subsequent web requests to maintain sessions or track browsing activity across domains.
  • Web Storage (localStorage): A modern HTML5 browser feature that allows websites to store key-value data locally inside your browser. Unlike cookies, data stored in localStorage stays strictly on your physical device and is never automatically sent over the network to the web server.

6. How to Manage or Clear Your Storage

You have full control over data stored on your device:

    • Safari (iOS / macOS): Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.
    • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
