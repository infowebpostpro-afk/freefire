Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026 Transparency & Data Practices
Key Summary: FreeFireNicknamePro.com is an independent gaming identity and text styling utility. All nickname generators, symbol pickers, and styling tools operate entirely inside your web browser using client-side code. We do not transmit, log, or store your nickname queries on external servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeFireNicknamePro.com ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website, use our interactive nickname generators, browse symbols, or communicate with us.

FreeFireNicknamePro.com is an independent third-party gaming utility website created for Free Fire players and gaming enthusiasts. We are dedicated to providing useful naming tools while respecting the privacy and autonomy of our visitors.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver and maintain our online utilities. Depending on how you interact with our website, information falls into two main categories:

Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you contact us directly via our Contact Us page or email, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name or alias
  • Your email address
  • The subject and content of your message or inquiry

We do not maintain user accounts, require user registration, or offer paid services. We do not collect credit card numbers, passwords, billing addresses, or in-game account credentials.

Information Collected Automatically

When you browse our website, standard web server logs or hosting infrastructure may automatically record basic technical data, such as:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type, language, and operating system
  • Referring website addresses and navigation paths
  • Date, time, and duration of page requests

This technical data is processed at the server level primarily to maintain network security, diagnose connection errors, and ensure reliable page delivery. FreeFireNicknamePro.com does not use this data to personally identify individual visitors.

3. Nickname Tool Inputs & Client-Side Processing

Client-Side Processing Notice: Every nickname generator, font converter, invisible space copy tool, bio generator, and symbol picker on FreeFireNicknamePro.com runs locally on your device via JavaScript.

When you type a seed word, existing name, initial, or custom tag into our tool fields (such as on our generator, boys nickname studio, or girls nickname studio):

  • Your input is processed strictly in your local browser's memory.
  • Your input is never transmitted to any backend server or remote database.
  • We do not record, log, sell, or analyze your nickname searches or generated results.

4. How Information Is Used

Any information we receive is used strictly for legitimate, transparent purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and ensure the stability of the website
  • To respond to your feedback, questions, or content correction requests
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate automated spam, abuse, or security threats
  • To evaluate general site performance and optimize mobile responsiveness

5. Cookies and Local Storage

FreeFireNicknamePro.com does not use first-party tracking or advertising cookies. However, to make our interactive tools convenient and personalized, our website uses standard browser localStorage on your device.

Specifically, localStorage is used to remember your shortlisted nicknames, favorited symbols, and recently copied items so you don't lose them when refreshing the page. This data remains on your device and is never sent to our servers.

For complete technical details about how localStorage and web storage work on our site, please review our comprehensive Cookie Policy.
