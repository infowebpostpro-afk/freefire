What Is a Free Fire Bio & How Do Color Codes Work?

Free Fire Profile Signature

Unlike your player nickname (which has a strict 12-character limit and restricts formatting), your profile signature field allows longer phrases, clan war declarations, social tags, and custom styling.

Free Fire Hex Color Codes

Text colors are created by prepending a 6-character hexadecimal code enclosed in square brackets (e.g. [FF0000] for red or [00E5FF] for cyan). The game engine renders any characters following that bracket in the chosen color.

Honest Compatibility & Game Version Note

Free Fire and Free Fire MAX updates periodically revise how special characters and BBCode bracket tags are parsed. We provide a Safe Plain Text copy fallback alongside all styled codes so that you never leave with a broken profile.
